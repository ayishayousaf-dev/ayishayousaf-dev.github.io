AI Telco Customer Churn Dashboard

A 3-page Power BI report that shows who is leaving, who is at risk, and who to call first.

85.7%Model accuracy
0.93ROC-AUC
1,408High-risk customers flagged
Churn overviewChurn rate by contract, tenure, customer value and monthly charges, with slicers.
Risk & retentionLow, medium and high risk segments by internet service, payment method and contract.
AI churn predictionA logistic regression model scores each customer. High risk is above 60% churn probability.

Built on 7,043 telecom customer records with an 80/20 stratified train/test split and 68.2% churn recall. I also trained a Random Forest for comparison (84.9% accuracy, 0.91 ROC-AUC). Logistic Regression scored higher and powers the dashboard. Average predicted churn probability is 26.38%, so retention teams can target the customers most likely to leave.
